Highlighter Calendar · Booking date field

Tap the date field and it grows into a calendar. Just swipe across the days you want, like using a highlighter.

  • Left-drag / swipe over the days: paint. A day counts as selected once 35/64 of its cells are painted; right-drag is the eraser, and tapping a day twice wipes its ink off.
  • Press and hold: the ink bleeds out and darkens; hold long enough and the day is selected in one sweep. Scrubbing back and forth deepens the color up to a limit.
  • Paper calendar: when expanded it is an open calendar book with one month on each page, and every sheet has a month printed on both sides. Only the days, varnished glossy, take the highlighter; the soft-touch matte paper round them (the title, the blank cells, the margin) is for holding the page. Press on it and drag left to turn the right page with your finger; drag right to turn the left page back. A tap on the matte paper turns that page, and quick repeated swipes flip page after page. On a phone it opens full screen: held upright, the months sit one above the other and the pages turn up and down (push the lower page up, pull the upper page down); held sideways, it opens side by side.
  • Bookmarks: any month with selected days gets a bookmark sticking out from the page edge, showing how many days are selected that month (1 to 5+). Tap a bookmark to jump straight to that month, and the bookmark tucks back into the page. Days already past are printed in grey.
  • Date field: once you pick, it shows the date range; with several ranges it becomes a carousel you can spin by dragging sideways, scrolling, or tapping the arrows on either side. Tap a range to open the calendar at its month.
