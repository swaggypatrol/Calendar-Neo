Highlighter Calendar · Booking date field

Tap the date field and it grows into a calendar. Just swipe across the days you want, like using a highlighter.

  • Left-drag / swipe: paint. A day counts as selected once 35/64 of its cells are painted; right-drag is the eraser.
  • Press and hold: the ink bleeds out and darkens; hold long enough and the day is selected in one sweep. Scrubbing back and forth deepens the color up to a limit.
  • Paper calendar: when expanded it is an open calendar book with one month on each page, and every sheet has a month printed on both sides. Press on empty space outside the date cells and drag left to turn the right page with your finger; drag right to turn the left page back. You can also tap the folded page corner, and quick repeated swipes flip page after page.
  • Bookmarks: any month with selected days gets a bookmark sticking out from the page edge, showing how many days are selected that month (1 to 5+). Tap a bookmark to jump straight to that month, and the bookmark tucks back into the page. Past months are printed with a dark vignette.
  • Date field: once you pick, it shows the date range; with several ranges it becomes a carousel you can spin by dragging sideways, scrolling, or tapping the arrows on either side. Tap a range to open the calendar at its month.
