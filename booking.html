Booking Calendar

For customer bookings: past days are greyed out, the pen leaves no ink on them and cannot select them, so only today and later can be booked.

  • Left-drag / swipe: paint to select; a day is booked once 35/64 of its cells are painted. It is fine to sweep from last week straight through to next week: past days are skipped automatically.
  • Right-drag: eraser, cancels a booking.
  • Press and hold: hold on a day long enough and it is selected in one sweep; holding between today and yesterday never bleeds into yesterday.
  • Multiple months: press on empty space on the card (such as the header) and drag sideways, and the card follows your finger. Drag left and the card slides away as next month comes out of the stack; drag right and the previous month slides in from the left. Up to two months show side by side. The current month is the earliest, so dragging right only moves a little with resistance and springs back on release. Quick repeated swipes spin through several months like a dial. Tap empty space to collapse back to a single card.
