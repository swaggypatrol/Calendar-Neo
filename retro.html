Retro Calculator Calendar

The opposite of the highlighter: exactly one date, picked by pressing a key on an old desk calculator, remade in liquid glass.

  • Every day is a key. Press one and it latches down; whichever key was down springs back up, like the interlocking station buttons on an old radio. Pressing the key that is already down does nothing.
  • Liquid glass. The body is clear glass that blurs the view behind it and bends it near its rounded edge; the keys are frosted glass. The bending needs Chromium; elsewhere the glass only blurs. Every corner is Apple's continuous (G2) curve and every proportion golden.
  • The filament display shows the chosen date in sunflower-yellow seven-segment filaments, with the weekday lit beneath. Filaments heat fast and cool slowly, so changing digits glow down rather than blink out.
  • Function keys: C releases the key that is down, the arrows turn the month (a sideways two-finger swipe on a trackpad does too), and Today brings back this month.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys walk the days (into the next month at the edges), Page Up / Page Down turn the month, Space or Enter presses a key.
  • Days outside min / max are locked: the key gives only a hair. A small lamp marks today.
