Retro Calculator Calendar · Booking date field

The opposite of the highlighter: exactly one date, picked on an old desk calculator remade in liquid glass. Tap the date field and the calculator slides out of it.

  • Every day is a key. Press one and it latches down, and whichever key was down springs back up, like the interlocking station buttons on an old radio. The date appears in the field the moment the key latches, and the lamp over that day's column lights up. Press the key that is down again and it springs up too, clearing the date.
  • Month and year: tap the month at the top and the days sink into the glass while twelve month keys rise in their place; the arrows then turn the year. Press a month and its days come back.
  • The arrows turn the month (a sideways two-finger swipe on a trackpad does too), and Today brings back this month.
  • Liquid glass. The body is clear glass that blurs the page behind it and bends it near its rounded edge; the keys are frosted glass. The bending needs Chromium; elsewhere the glass only blurs. Every corner is Apple's continuous (G2) curve and every proportion golden.
  • Keyboard: Enter or the down arrow on the field opens it; arrow keys walk the days (into the next month at the edges), Page Up / Page Down turn the month, Space or Enter presses a key, Esc puts the days back or closes it.
  • Days outside min / max are locked: the key gives only a hair. A small lamp marks today.
